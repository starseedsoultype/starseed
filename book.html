Just Because

She Wanted to Die Happy

One evening I opened an AI chat and typed a complaint: no one comes to me. A question came back, then another. Answering them, I went down the women's line of my family. My mother's mother, whose husband left her for her own sister. My mother, left with three children and not a single piece of furniture. A five-year-old girl who built her a sofa out of four chairs. A father's passport with no record of his children. A white bird curled up under the seat of a car. At the very bottom, three words: for no reason. Everything in this book happened.
