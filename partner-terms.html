1. Joining the Program

Anyone aged 18 or over may apply through the partners page. We review every application personally and may accept or decline it at our discretion. Once accepted, you receive a unique promo code. Your code gives your audience 10% off the Full Origin Reading at Gumroad checkout.

2. Your Earnings

You earn a flat $6 for every qualifying purchase. A qualifying purchase is a Full Origin Reading bought with your code that stays paid.

The following purchases do not qualify:

  • Purchases that are refunded or charged back

If the price of the Full Origin Reading or the amount you earn changes, we will tell you by email at least 30 days in advance. Earnings from purchases made before the change stay as they were.

3. Payouts

We pay on the 11th of every month for all qualifying purchases of the previous calendar month. If the 11th falls on a weekend or holiday, payment is made on the next business day. Before each payout you receive a short statement with the number of qualifying purchases and your amount.

Payouts are sent by PayPal or Wise to an account in your own name. Please keep your payout details accurate. We pay our side of the transfer fee; fees charged by your own PayPal or Wise account, or by currency conversion, are yours.

If a purchase is refunded after it was paid out, we may deduct that amount from your next payout.
