What happens in the session

    His name, when it started, what actually happened between you. Dates and behaviour, in your own words, at your own pace.

Questions women ask me first

Am I imagining all of this?

This is the first question almost every woman asks, which tells you how common it is. What you feel has a structure, and I describe that structure. You leave with a clear account of it rather than reassurance.

We have barely spoken in years. Is there enough to read?

Yes. The reading works from who the two of you are, so silence, distance and years apart leave it fully readable. Many of the women I read have been out of contact for a long time.

Will you tell me whether he comes back?

I describe how your connection is built and how it behaves. Predictions belong to a different trade, and I leave them there. What you gain is understanding precise enough to make your own decisions with a clear head.

What if I go blank and lose my words?

That happens often, and it is fine. I ask a few simple questions and the picture becomes clear quickly. Forty five minutes leaves plenty of room, and the written reading carries the rest.

Is this therapy?

This is a reading of one connection, delivered by someone trained in psychology. For treatment of a mental health condition, a therapist in your own country is the right person, and I will say so plainly if that is what I hear.
