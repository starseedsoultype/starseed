2. Information We Collect

2.1 Through Our Payment Processors

Purchases are processed by Gumroad Inc. Some purchases are processed by Stripe. When you make a purchase, the payment processor collects and processes your payment information, email address, and transaction data. We receive your email address in order to deliver your purchased product, provide customer support, and send you updates about new StarSeedSoul products and events.

You may opt out of these emails at any time by contacting us at timsocion@gmail.com.

We do not collect or store your payment card details. These are handled entirely by the payment processor.

Their privacy policies apply to all payment transactions: gumroad.com/privacy and stripe.com/privacy

2.2 Through Telegram Bot

When you interact with our Telegram bot (@SeedSoulTest_bot) to receive a free PDF, your Telegram username is visible to us through the Telegram platform. We do not store or process this data beyond what is necessary to deliver the requested file.

Telegram's Privacy Policy applies: telegram.org/privacy

2.3 Through Calendly (Session Booking)

When you book a personal reading session, scheduling is handled through Calendly. Calendly collects your name, email address, and time zone in order to facilitate the booking. We receive this information to confirm and conduct your session.

Calendly's Privacy Policy applies: calendly.com/pages/privacy

2.4 Through Your Account and the Quizzes

Parts of this website let you create an account, take a quiz, receive a reading, build a profile, and connect with other members. This data is stored in our database, which is hosted by Supabase on servers located in the United States.

Depending on which parts you use, this may include:

  • Your email address and login credentials
  • Your quiz answers and the reading generated from them
  • Your profile details and any photo you upload
  • Your likes, matches, and the messages you send inside the platform

Messages you send to other members are visible to those members. We can access this data as the operator of the platform, and we access it only to run the service, provide support, and respond to reports of abuse.

You may request deletion of your account and its data at any time by writing to timsocion@gmail.com.

Supabase's Privacy Policy applies to this hosting: supabase.com/privacy

2.5 Through Forms

When you register for a live workshop or event, or send us a request through a form on the website, we collect your name and email address along with anything you choose to write. Some of these forms are delivered to us by Web3Forms. This information is used solely to answer you and to communicate event details.

Web3Forms' Privacy Policy applies to those submissions: web3forms.com/privacy

2.6 Cookies, Analytics, and the Website Itself

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may retain standard server logs as part of its hosting infrastructure.

We use the following measurement tools:

  • Meta Pixel. Most pages of this website load the Meta Pixel. It uses cookies and tells Meta that you visited a given page and which actions you took there, such as opening a quiz or clicking through to a purchase. Meta may use this to show you our content later. You can control this in your Meta account settings and through your browser's cookie controls.
  • Google Analytics 4. The Clarity Session page uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits, clicks, and completed bookings. It is configured to store nothing on your device, so it sets no cookies and does not recognise you across visits.

Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts and some code libraries from public content delivery networks. These providers receive your IP address as a technical consequence of serving those files.

Booking and payment pages are provided by Calendly, Gumroad, and Stripe, which set their own cookies once you interact with them.

2.7 Direct Communication

If you contact us by email, Instagram, Telegram, or any other channel, we retain that correspondence in order to respond to you and provide support.
