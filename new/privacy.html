2.4.1 Oli, the AI Guide

Oli is an AI guide, not a person. You can talk to Oli in the TwinF app or on her own page, twinfrequency.io/oli; both are the same conversation, kept in the same account. On her own page Oli asks for your agreement before anything you write is sent to the AI, and you can delete your account yourself from the menu there.

When you write to Oli, or mention @Oli in a circle, your message and the recent messages of that conversation are sent to OpenAI, which generates Oli's reply. OpenAI processes this data on our behalf on servers in the United States. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train its models.

So that a conversation can continue over time, Oli remembers a few things you choose to share: how you refer to the people you talk about, their Origin if you name it or find it together with Oli, and short notes about what you told her. When you ask Oli for a relationship reading, your request, your name and the two Origins are passed to Alexandra so she can prepare it personally.

Messages to Oli are also checked automatically for signs that someone may be in danger. In that case Oli replies with information about where to find immediate help instead of a reading.

All of this is stored in our database together with your account and is deleted when your account is deleted. You can also ask us to delete only what Oli remembers by writing to timsocion@gmail.com.

2.4.2 Oli Safety

Oli is an AI companion made for adults aged 18 and over. Oli is an AI, and this is said on the login page, in the agreement before the first message and in Oli's first message.

Crisis protocol. Every message to Oli is checked for signs of suicidal thoughts, self-harm or danger from another person, both by a list of phrases and by an automatic moderation model that reads every language. When such signs appear, Oli sends a prepared message instead of an AI reply. It points to the local emergency number, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the US (call or text), Samaritans on 116 123 in the UK and Ireland, and findahelpline.com for free, confidential lines in every other country. This works in private chats and in circles, and also before a person has agreed to the AI.

Under 18. Before the first conversation on Oli's page, a person confirms they are 18 or older. If someone tells Oli they are under 18, Oli ends the conversation with a prepared message that points them to a trusted adult and to help lines, and answers any later message the same way.

What we count. To report to the California Office of Suicide Prevention as the law requires, we count how many of these prepared messages were sent, by date only, with no names, accounts or message text.

Oli supports reflection on relationships. For medical or psychological care, please reach a qualified professional.

2.7 Cookies, Analytics, and the Website Itself

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may retain standard server logs as part of its hosting infrastructure.

We use the following measurement tools:

  • Google Analytics 4. Pages of this website use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and actions such as opening the Origin Scan, reaching a result, booking a session, or clicking through to a purchase. It is configured to store nothing on your device, so it sets no cookies and does not recognise you across visits.
  • TikTok Pixel. Pages of this website load the TikTok Pixel. It uses cookies and tells TikTok that you visited a given page and which actions you took there, such as reaching a scan result, booking a session, or clicking through to a purchase. TikTok may use this to measure our ads and show you our content later. If your browser is set to a time zone in the European Union, the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the TikTok Pixel loads only after you press Accept in the cookie banner, and your choice is remembered in your browser. You can also control this in your TikTok account settings and through your browser's cookie controls.

Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts and some code libraries from public content delivery networks. These providers receive your IP address as a technical consequence of serving those files.

Booking and payment pages are provided by Cal.com, Gumroad, and Stripe, which set their own cookies once you interact with them.
